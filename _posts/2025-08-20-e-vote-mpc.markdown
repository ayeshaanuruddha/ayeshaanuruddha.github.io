<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This project tackles the challenges of manual, paper-based electoral systems in Sri Lanka by proposing a modern, cost-effective, privacy-preserving electronic voting framework. It leverages edge computing and Multi-Party Computation (MPC) to guarantee the Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Authentication, and Non-repudiation (CIAAN) of every vote. By demonstrating successful biometric authentication and secure distributed tallying, this prototype establishes a verifiable and trust-building architecture for national elections.</p>

<br>
<br>

<h3>Edge-Based E-Voting System with MPC</h3>

<br>
<br>

<h4>📋 Project Overview</h4>
<table class="table table-bordered">
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>🎯 <strong>Objective</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">To establish a verifiable, trust-building e-voting system tailored for Sri Lanka's infrastructural constraints.</td></tr>

<br>

<hr>

<h3>(1) Core System Architecture</h3>
<table class="table table-bordered">
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><strong>Tier 1: Edge Nodes</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">ESP32 devices handle local biometric voter authentication and ballot encryption at polling stations to reduce network dependence.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Tier 2: MPC Overlay</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">A secure peer-to-peer network distributes secret shares of the votes to enable collective tallying without revealing individual selections.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Tier 3: Central Services</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">A high-performance FastAPI backend manages the voter registry, API routing, and final encrypted result storage via MySQL.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<br>

<h3>(2) Key Implementation Features</h3>
<table class="table table-bordered table-striped">
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><strong>Biometric Authentication</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">Voters are authenticated using fingerprint scanners integrated with ESP32 edge nodes, ensuring the one-person-one-vote principle.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Secure Vote Casting</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">Votes are cryptographically encapsulated using advanced protocols before storage to ensure absolute integrity and secrecy.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Distributed Tallying</strong></td><td style="text-align: justify;">MPC protocols guarantee that it is technologically impossible for any single authority to tamper with or reconstruct individual votes.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<br>

<h3>(3) System Evaluation & Future Work</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">A developed local prototype successfully demonstrated the complete pipeline: fingerprint-based voter authentication, secure ballot encryption, and distributed MPC tallying. While testing successfully proved the feasibility of preventing duplicate voting and protecting against tampering, it was conducted in a controlled environment limited to a single ESP32 device, meaning high-load scalability remains to be fully evaluated.</p>

<br>

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Future Enhancements:</strong> The roadmap for a nationwide rollout includes extending the prototype to higher-performance edge devices (like the Raspberry Pi), integrating post-quantum cryptography (PQC) for long-term security, and employing multi-biometric sensors for enhanced authentication.</p>
