(1) Business Context & User Story

As a Corporate Revenue Accountant

I want inbound POS card transaction webhooks to automatically unbundle gross sales into merchandise revenue, sales tax liabilities, and merchant processing fees, posting balanced double-entry journals into the cloud ERP

So that I recognize real-time revenue and operational fee expenses immediately at the point of sale, eliminate manual spreadsheet journal uploads during month-end close, and mathematically prevent unbalanced financial postings from reaching the general ledger.

Operational Elicitation & System Requirements

DimensionAs-Is FindingTo-Be Contract
Fee RecognitionMerchant fees (2.6% + $0.10) deducted as a net lump-sum payout days later, forcing weekly estimates.Middleware parses fee_breakdown.amount and debits GL 6200 — Payment Processing Fees immediately.
Tax SegregationStore managers report gross totals; staff manually split tax in Excel, causing cent-level errors.Middleware extracts tax_breakdown.total and credits GL 2200 — Sales Tax Payable.
Ledger IntegrityManual CSV journal uploads fail 3.8% of the time due to rounding differences.Pre-commit assertion enforces Σ Debits − Σ Credits = $0.00 before ERP dispatch.

Inbound Webhook Contract

{
  "event": "payment.captured",
  "gross_amount": "5.40",
  "tax_breakdown": { "total": "0.40" },
  "fee_breakdown": { "amount": "0.24" },
  "net_settlement": "5.16",
  "currency": "USD"
}

(2) Functional Scenario — Nominal Path

GIVEN
a retail customer purchases an item ($5.00 base + $0.40 sales tax = $5.40 total) and POS captures payment
WHEN
the payment gateway dispatches the payment.captured webhook: gross $5.40, tax $0.40, fee $0.24, net settlement $5.16
THEN
the middleware verifies Σ Debits ($5.16 + $0.24) − Σ Credits ($5.00 + $0.40) = $0.00 and submits an atomic journal entry to the ERP
