Beyond the enterprise systems.

This space is my digital sandbox — a library of Sinhala science fiction, speculative technical articles, and deep dives into pop culture, philosophy, and tech-noir.

Whether I am mapping out complex business requirements or constructing an intricate fictional universe, I thrive on turning chaotic ideas into structured, meaningful narratives.
